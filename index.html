I am a PhD candidate in International Relations at the University of St Andrews, affiliated with the Centre for Peace and Conflict Studies and the PeaceRep (MEND) project, under the supervision of Nicholas Barnes, Nick Brooke, and Elisa D’Amico. In 2026–27 I am a Graduate Fellow at the Program on Negotiation at Harvard University, and a Teaching Fellow for Robert Mnookin’s seminar How Wars End.

My research uses computational and quantitative methods to study how states respond to contentious politics: whether police treat protesters differently depending on their partisanship, whether environmental protest moves legislatures, and how climate policy ideas take shape inside EU institutions. Much of my work relies on text-as-data and NLP methods, including transformer models and LLM-based classification.
